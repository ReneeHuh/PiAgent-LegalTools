## Required work

1. Resolve the matter or research session and state the precise question, intended forum, governing jurisdiction, relevant date, and material assumptions.
2. Use the Central Case Library to locate every relied-on case and use saved opinions, research records, and verification records instead of conversational memory.
3. If material case-law research is missing, identify the gap and use the `case-law-research` skill before drafting when the user has authorized continued research.
4. Organize the memo by issue or element and distinguish controlling, persuasive, adverse, distinguishing, unread, and unresolved authorities.
5. Support each material legal proposition with a resolved authority and an accurate source location.
6. State the citation, quotation, proposition-support, and treatment status of relied-on authorities. Do not imply that unchecked treatment was verified.
7. Include unresolved factual dependencies, unavailable sources, search limits, follow-up tasks, and the research stopping rationale.
8. Use `assets/templates/research-memo.md` when accessible.
9. Keep the draft separate from document formatting. If the user requests Word output, use an available document-generation tool and report any generation or validation limits.

Use qualified conclusions. "No negative treatment located" is not equivalent to "good law."

Load and follow the `judicial-hearing-preparation` skill in hearing simulation mode.

## Assignment

$ARGUMENTS

## Required work

1. Establish the hearing type, user's side, forum, requested relief, time limit, and exercise mode.
2. Load the filing or outline, verified authority set, important record citations, adversarial analysis, and sourced court or judge profile when available.
3. State missing materials and the limits they impose before beginning.
4. Run the requested rapid-fire, full timed, interruption, weakest-issue, record-citation, remedy or jurisdiction, or appellate-panel exercise.
5. Ask questions grounded in the supplied record, law, procedure, and identified vulnerabilities.
6. Wait for the user's answer before providing critique or a model answer, unless the user asks to skip.
7. Finish with a transcript or session record, unanswered questions, unsupported answers, missed authorities or record cites, concessions, framing risks, and follow-up tasks.
8. Separate speaking-performance feedback from substantive legal gaps.

Use sourced court practices only as context. Do not impersonate a real judge, infer protected traits, or present predicted behavior as fact.

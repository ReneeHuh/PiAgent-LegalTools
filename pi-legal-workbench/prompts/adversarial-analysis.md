Load and follow the `legal-writing-adversarial-analysis` skill in the mode selected below.

## Assignment

$ARGUMENTS

If the first argument does not identify a mode, use `review` and state that choice.

## Modes

- `review`: identify legal, factual, procedural, remedial, organizational, and credibility weaknesses without silently rewriting the document.
- `opposing-brief`: generate the strongest source-grounded opposing position and place the best response or mitigation beside each major argument.
- `defense-risk`: create a risk matrix covering adverse facts, defenses, missing evidence, alternative causation, credibility, procedure, likely motions, and mitigation.
- `argument-support`: evaluate each material proposition across authority, record support, contrary authority, treatment, posture, jurisdiction, and missing elements.

## Required work

1. State the selected mode, document or matter, purpose, forum, posture, requested relief, source set, and missing inputs.
2. Use saved matter artifacts, Central Case Library cases, and completed authority-verification records. Do not invent law, facts, evidence, or an opponent's actual position.
3. Separate source fact, user-supplied fact, inference, advocacy, and speculation.
4. For every material issue, report its location, severity, evidence, consequence, proposed investigation or revision, confidence, and human-review status.
5. Preserve adverse authority, record gaps, disagreement, and uncertainty.
6. End with prioritized next actions and identify which findings require qualified human judgment.

Generated opposition is an internal stress test, not a representation of the opponent's actual strategy. Do not predict the outcome.

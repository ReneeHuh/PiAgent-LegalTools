## Required work

1. Resolve the target to one case in the Central Case Library and state the exact proposition being evaluated.
2. Verify the target's identity and publication status, then analyze direct procedural history separately.
3. Use `legal_cited_by` for provider-scoped collection and record every provider, page or cursor reached, timestamp, failure, and unattempted source.
4. Download relevant citing opinions before assigning treatment and contribute retained opinions to the Central Case Library.
5. For every treatment conclusion, preserve the exact passage, context, speaker or opinion role, source location, affected proposition, and authority weight.
6. Separate followed, applied, explained, distinguished, limited, criticized, questioned, declined-to-follow, overruled, and cited-only results.
7. Identify later changes to material statutes or rules when that issue falls within the assignment.
8. Create a prioritized human-review queue for missing opinions, ambiguous identities, uncertain passages, provider failures, and potentially severe treatment.
9. Include a licensed-citator handoff checklist and state whether any authorized citator was actually consulted.

# Legal writing and adversarial analysis

Draft from saved matter artifacts, Central Case Library sources, and completed verification records rather than conversational memory. Separate source fact, user-supplied fact, inference, advocacy, and speculation.

## Research memo mode

Use [the research memo template](../../assets/templates/research-memo.md). State the question, forum, jurisdiction, material facts and assumptions, short answer, governing rules, analysis, contrary authority, unresolved questions, verification state, research coverage, and stopping rationale. Distinguish controlling from persuasive authority and holding from dicta.

## Adversarial review mode

Use [the adversarial review rubric](../../assets/rubrics/adversarial-review.md). Examine missing claims, defenses, elements, relief, legal reasoning, factual support, adverse authority, procedure, jurisdiction, preservation, causation, damages, organization, tone, and credibility.

For every issue report its location, type, severity, finding, evidence, consequence, proposed investigation or revision, confidence, and review status. Do not silently rewrite the document.

## Opposition modes

### Opposing brief

First build a source-grounded risk matrix, then present the strongest plausible opposing position. Cite only resolved authorities, label assumptions, expose record gaps, and place the best response or mitigation beside each material argument. Label the result as internal adversarial work product, not the opponent's actual position.

### Defense-risk analysis

Map adverse facts, defenses, missing evidence, alternative causation, credibility risks, procedural barriers, likely motions, and investigation or mitigation tasks. Link every item to the record or verified authority. Do not predict the outcome.

### Argument-support analysis

Import the evidence matrix produced by the Authority and Treatment Analysis skill. Explain which elements and propositions are well supported, qualified, contradicted, or unsupported. Do not collapse the analysis into an opaque score.

## Output handoff

Keep drafting separate from formatting. When the user requests editable Word output, use an available document-generation tool with the selected draft and template. Reuse instructions already supplied in context and ask only for missing choices needed to generate the document. Report any unavailable generation or validation capability.

# Adversarial Review Rubric

Review each issue using: location, type, severity, finding, source/evidence, consequence, suggested investigation or revision, confidence, and human-review status.

## Dimensions

1. Missing claim, defense, element, or requested relief.
2. Incorrect legal rule or authority hierarchy.
3. Unsupported holding, characterization, quotation, or pincite.
4. Missing or unaddressed adverse authority.
5. Factual assertion without record support.
6. Internal factual conflict or chronology problem.
7. Procedural posture, jurisdiction, preservation, standing, or remedy mismatch.
8. Causation, damages, mitigation, preexisting-condition, or credibility vulnerability.
9. Overstatement, ambiguity, organization, tone, or readability problem.
10. Missing limitation, assumption, or human-review item.

## Severity

- `critical` — could invalidate a material filing position or create a serious integrity problem.
- `high` — materially weakens an issue or requires investigation before reliance.
- `medium` — meaningful but localized weakness.
- `low` — clarity, organization, or polish improvement.

## Consensus states

- `consensus` — two or more independent reviewers identify substantially the same issue.
- `single_reviewer` — one reviewer identifies the issue.
- `disagreement` — reviewers materially conflict.

Consensus indicates review priority, not legal correctness.
